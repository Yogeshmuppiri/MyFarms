Acceptance of terms

By creating a My Farms account, signing in, placing an order, using wallet coins, or contacting support, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. You must provide accurate information and must not use the service for false accounts, fraudulent orders, promo misuse, misleading complaints, unauthorized access, or activity that harms customers, employees, farmers, delivery partners, or My Farms systems.

Account data

Name, email, phone, and login details

We store your name, email address, phone number, account ID, encrypted password, and profile details to create your account, verify access, send service messages, and protect the platform from malicious or unauthorized activity.

Retention: stored until you delete your account or request deletion. Security logs or limited records may be retained when required to prevent fraud, investigate abuse, comply with law, or protect business records.

Communication

Email, phone calls, and service updates

My Farms may email you for signup verification, password reset, order confirmation, delivery assignment, delivery status, complaint updates, account security, and important service notices. We may call or message the phone number on your account when needed for delivery, pickup, payment coordination, support, or safety checks.

Retention: email and support message records may be kept with your account, order, or complaint record.

Orders

Cart, delivery address, payment option, and order history

We store order items, quantity, selected variants, order total, promo code usage, wallet coin activity, payment option, delivery address, pickup selection, and status timeline so we can prepare, assign, deliver, support, and audit your order.

Retention: customer order history is available for up to 6 months. Active orders remain visible until the order is completed. Delivered orders may remain in the recent tracker for 48 hours, then move to order history.

Delivery

Delivery assignment and tracking

When an order requires delivery, we may store delivery assignment details, assigned employee, delivery status, timestamps, and status updates so supervisors and delivery agents can complete the order.

Retention: delivery-agent personal assignment views may be cleared after 60 days. Company order records and dashboard data may be retained up to 6 months or converted to non-personal summary data.

Support

Complaints and proof photos

If you report an order issue, we may store your order number, issue description, support messages, uploaded proof photos, status, replies, and timestamps to investigate and resolve the complaint.

Retention: complaint details and proof photos are normally retained for up to 30 days after resolution, unless a longer period is needed for fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal compliance, or business protection.

Freshness and assistant tools

Photos, voice, and app tools

Optional app tools such as freshness checks, proof-photo upload, voice commands, or assistant actions are used only to provide the requested feature. Camera or image uploads are processed for the selected action, and proof images are stored only when submitted with a complaint.

Retention: optional analysis inputs are not used as account identity records. Submitted complaint proof follows the complaint retention period.
