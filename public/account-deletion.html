Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your My Farms account

My Farms customers can request deletion of their account and associated personal data from the mobile app or by contacting us directly.

Account Profile and login access removed
Orders Personal details removed after retention period
Support Contact owner for help with deletion

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the My Farms app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Edit Profile.
  4. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your customer account access and deletes personal profile data such as name, email, phone number, saved profile details, wallet records tied to the account, and active login sessions.

Retention

Data we may keep temporarily

Some order, delivery, complaint, refund, or transaction records may be retained for business, safety, legal, fraud-prevention, and accounting purposes. Customer-facing order history is limited to recent records, and older personal operational details are removed according to My Farms retention rules.

After retention ends, personal identifiers are deleted or de-identified while non-personal dashboard metrics may be kept for business reporting.
