About My Farms

Purely Rooted, Honestly Delivered

At My Farms, we believe that the best food does not come from a factory or a long corporate supply chain. It comes from the earth, tended with care, and delivered with honesty. Along with our own farms, we collaborate with trusted farmers who share our standards so families can receive excellent-quality food consistently.

Our Philosophy: 99.7% Raw & Unrefined

Most products marketed as healthy still go through heavy processing and long storage cycles. We do things differently. Our goal is to keep food as close to nature as possible so nutrients, enzymes, aroma, and flavor stay naturally present. To keep delivery reliable through seasonal changes, we also work with selected farmer partners who follow the same quality-first approach.

Our Promise

The Direct-from-Farmer Promise

  • We directly manage our core farms and monitor quality at each stage.
  • We collaborate only with trusted farmers who follow strict growing and handling standards.
  • Our farmer network helps us continue deliveries without lowering freshness or quality.
  • No unnecessary middle layers means fresher produce and faster delivery.

Why It Matters

Why Organic Farming Is Important

  • Protects soil fertility and biodiversity for future generations.
  • Reduces chemical load in food and supports better long-term health.
  • Improves water quality by reducing synthetic runoff.
  • Strengthens local farm communities with sustainable livelihoods.

Clarity

What Is the Remaining 0.3%?

The 0.3% refers to unavoidable practical handling steps like cleaning, sorting, moisture stabilization, hygienic packaging, and safe transport. It is not aggressive industrial processing and not chemical refinement.

Traditional Craft

Bull-Driven Organic Groundnut Oil

Our traditional bull-driven method uses slow mechanical extraction with low heat. Groundnuts are pressed gently in a wooden churn system, helping preserve natural aroma and micronutrients that are often lost in high-heat industrial refining. No chemical solvents, no artificial polishing, and no shortcuts.
